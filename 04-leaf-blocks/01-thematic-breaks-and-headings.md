# 4. Leaf Blocks: Thematic Breaks and Headings

## Thematic Breaks
A line consisting of up to three spaces of indentation, followed by three or more matching `-`, `_`, or `*` characters forms a thematic break.
```markdown
***
---
___
```

## Headings
### ATX Headings
Consists of 1-6 `#` characters followed by a space.
```markdown
# Heading 1
## Heading 2
```

### Setext Headings
Consists of text underlined by `=` (for Level 1) or `-` (for Level 2).
```markdown
Heading 1
=========

Heading 2
---------
```
